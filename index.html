Data tools

Your data never leaves this device

  • No account, no sign-up. payrecordpro.app only delivers this page to you — nothing you type is ever sent back, uploaded, shared, or sold. (Like any website, the host can see that the page was requested, but never what you enter.)
  • Your pay, taxes and history are stored only in this browser's local storage on this device.
  • This page tells your browser to block it from making connections or sending forms, so it technically cannot send your data anywhere. Installed as an app, it even works offline.
  • No ads, analytics, trackers or cookies.
  • Backups are files you export yourself — they go where you put them, nowhere else.
Heads-up: clearing your browser's site data erases what's saved here. Use “Export backup” in History to keep a copy. Tax numbers are rough US estimates, not tax advice.

Your money stays on your device

Before you enter anything, here's the deal:

  • Everything you enter is saved only on this device, in this browser.
  • It is never sent anywhere — not to payrecordpro.app, not to anyone. No account, no tracking. The page even tells your browser to block it from sending anything anywhere.
  • You can export or erase everything at any time.
